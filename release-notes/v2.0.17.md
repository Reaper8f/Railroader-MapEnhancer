# Release Notes — Railroader Map Enhancer v2.0.17

**Release Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Target Game Version:** Railroader 0.6.0+ Experimental  
**Packages:** `reaper-mapenhancer-2.0.17.zip` (Complete), `reaper-mapenhancer-2.0.17-nexus.zip` (Nexus Edition)  
**Mod Identifier:** `reaper/map-enhancer`

---

## 🌟 Major Highlights & New Features

Map Enhancer **v2.0.17** delivers a major upgrade to map cartography, telemetry presentation, and automated dispatching. It introduces the **3rd offline corridor map tile pack (`USGSImageryTopo`)** combining NAIP satellite aerial imagery with USGS topographic contours & place labels, **3 selectable Hover HUD display modes** including a detached, resizable **Inspection HUD window** with `Ctrl+H` toggle keybind, **intelligent coupling-point waypoint dispatch**, **Track Class/Condition/Speed & Weed Growth hover telemetry**, **Condition overlay metric parity**, and **Linux/SteamOS packaging fixes**.

---

### 1. 🛰️ USGS Satellite & Topo Corridor Tile Pack (`USGSImageryTopo`)

Expand your map backgrounds with high-resolution USDA NAIP aerial orthoimagery layered with USGS elevation contours, mountain summits, hydrography, roads, and station place-name annotations:

- **3rd Built-In Provider (`USGSImageryTopo`):** Automatically discovered when extracted into `Tiles\USGSImageryTopo\` and selectable directly in Settings (`Ctrl+M`) as **USGS Satellite (Imagery & Topo)**.
- **Full Andrews-to-Sylva Corridor Coverage:** 6,040 validated PNG tiles across Zooms 12–16 (100% coverage, 0 missing or invalid tiles).
- **100% Offline Local Streaming:** Zero network requests during gameplay.
- **Updated `downloadtiles.bat` Downloader:** Includes option `[3]` for USGS Satellite & Topo (~926 MB), option `[4]` to install all 3 tile packs (~1.65 GB Total), and automated SHA256 verification.
- **Regenerated `USGSTopo` Archive:** Verified and fixed missing boundary tiles so `MapEnhancer_Tiles_USGSTopo_Andrews_to_Sylva.zip` now contains all 6,040 tiles cleanly.

---

### 2. 🖥️ 3 Hover HUD Modes & Reorganized Inspection HUD Window

Customize how live hover telemetry appears across the map:

1. **Attached to Map:** Fixed in the map window corner (classic minimal view).
2. **Hover Window:** Floats dynamically beside the cursor across the map.
3. **Hover Pinned Window (Dedicated Inspection HUD):** Uses a native, resizable, movable Railroader UI window (`WindowManager`) whose position and size persist automatically. Retaining `[Last Inspected]` content allows studying long waybills or consist manifests without keeping your cursor hovered over a tiny marker.

**Organized Entity Layouts inside the Inspection HUD:**
- **Unified Locomotive, Tender & Consist View:** Hovering over either a locomotive **or** its tender displays **both** engine telemetry (Driver/AI, Mode, Speed, Coal/Water/Diesel %, Loco & Tender Handbrake, Loco & Tender Journal Oil, Hotbox alerts) **and** consist telemetry (Length, Total Weight, Cars Loaded/Empty, Passenger Service, Freight Manifest, Destinations, Consist Alerts) together.
- **Individual Freight Car View:** Waybill status badges (`DELIVERED`, `PENDING`, `EMPTY`), commodity, payload weight vs capacity, destination, handbrake, journal oil, and consist position (`Car X of Y`).
- **Track Segment View:** Geometry (grade %, uphill/downhill arrow, length in ft & m), effective & safe curving speed limits, FRA track class, per-class vs overall condition, and weed growth percentage.
- **Station & Player Sign View:** Up/down direction passenger counts and destinations, plus speed signs and track notes.

**Toolbar & Keybind Controls:**
- **Dynamic Toolbar Visibility:** The map toolbar `HUD` button only appears when **Hover Pinned Window** mode is active.
- **Inspection HUD Keybind (`Ctrl+H`):** Rebindable hotkey under *Keybindings & Shortcuts* to toggle the dedicated Inspection HUD window open/closed just like clicking the `HUD` button.

---

### 3. 🎯 Intelligent Waypoint Dispatch – Send to Couple

Upgraded click-to-couple navigation (`CapsLock + Click` on a car) to act as an intelligent coupling-route planner:

- **Coupling-Point Awareness:** Inspects both couplers (`End A` and `End B`) of the target car/consist and never routes into an occupied coupling point.
- **Optimal Route Selection & Run-Arounds:** Evaluates actual track distance and switch connectivity. If the direct end is blocked by adjacent rolling stock, the planner routes the locomotive past the target through available switches/turnouts to reverse and approach the open coupling end.
- **Live Route Preview Toggle & Keybind:** Added an in-game toggle and configurable shortcut for the real-time cyan dispatch route ribbon.

---

### 4. 🛤️ Track Hover Metrics & Condition Overlay Metric Parity

Resolved discrepancies where the Condition track overlay painted physical wear while hover tooltips displayed per-class target percentages:

- **New Hover Detail Toggles:**
  - *Track Class, Condition & Speed:* Displays FRA Class (1–5) and effective speed limit.
  - *Track Condition & Weed Growth:* Displays condition percentage and right-of-way weed clearing percentage.
- **Hover Track Metric Display:** Choose between *Per-Class Target (Relative)*, *Overall Physical (0–100%)*, or *Both (Overall & Relative)*.
- **Condition Overlay Metric:** Choose whether the Condition track overlay colors tracks by *Per-Class Target* or *Overall Physical (0–100%)*, with automatic synchronization of the floating map legend.

---

### 5. 🛠️ Infrastructure, Installers & Documentation

- **Linux / SteamOS Archive Normalization:** Fixed directory separator paths in release zips so `MapEnhancer` extracts cleanly on Linux and Steam Deck / Proton environments.
- **Multiplayer Client Mod Documentation:** Added step-by-step instructions to README, Install Guide, and Website on enabling Map Enhancer as a `clientMods` entry in `Preferences.json` during multiplayer sessions.
- **Updated `install.bat`:** Safer legacy folder removal and stricter protection for `settings.json`, `Backgrounds/`, and `Tiles/`.
