# Release Notes — Railroader Map Enhancer v2.0.16

**Release Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Target Game Version:** Railroader 0.6.2 Experimental  
**Packages:** `reaper-mapenhancer-2.0.16.zip` (Complete), `reaper-mapenhancer-2.0.16-nexus.zip` (Nexus Edition)

## 🌟 Major Highlights & New Features

Map Enhancer v2.0.16 is a massive feature and refinement update. It introduces **dynamic slippy map tile streaming** with authentic public-domain **USGS Topo** and **OpenStreetMap** overlays, **custom background image calibration**, a new **Physical Safe Speed Restriction overlay**, **CTC passing siding route propagation**, **sub-segment industry delivery slicing**, **upright player sign text**, **car marker length & width trimming**, **consist depth filing (shingling)**, **alternating consist contrast**, **selected equipment map highlighting**, and complete **user data protection** during packaging and deployment.

Bring the Great Smoky Mountains to life on your dispatch map with real-world topographic and cartographic overlays rendered directly beneath your tracks:

- **3 Background Modes with Dynamic Tile Set Selection:**
  1. **`Disabled (Default Map)`** — Clean, plain vanilla canvas background.
  2. **`Custom Image`** — Load historical track charts, aerial orthophotos, or custom artwork from `Mods/reaper-map-enhancer/Backgrounds/`. Supports automatic track boundary fitting or millimeter-accurate companion `.json` coordinate calibration.
  3. **`Map Tiles (Unified Local Tile Subsystem)`** _(Recommended)_ — High-performance, 100% local slippy map streaming subsystem. Automatically discovers tile sets in `Mods/reaper-map-enhancer/Tiles/<Folder>/`:
     - **`USGSTopo`**: Authentic Appalachian mountain topography, contour lines, elevations, hydrology, and historical railroad alignments (100% US Government Public Domain) across Zooms 12–16.
     - **`OSM`**: High-contrast GIS street, river, and yard cartography across Zooms 12–17, supporting both offline packs and automatic detection of Railroader's native GIS cache.
     - **Custom Tile Packs**: Any custom folder dropped into `Tiles/<Folder>/` is dynamically detected and listed in the in-game dropdown.
- **100% Local-Only Operation (Zero Network Overhead):**
  - Zero network requests are initiated by Map Enhancer. The tile subsystem operates completely offline directly from disk, eliminating rate-limiting, network lag, external tracking, or API dependencies.
- **Dynamic Viewport Camera Streaming & Pure Hysteresis:**
  - Seamlessly tracks map camera position and zoom level in real time.
  - Automatically transitions through an adaptive zoom ladder:
    - **Zoom 17** ($< 380\text{ m}$ camera size, $\approx 0.97\text{ m/px}$): Razor-sharp rail yard switching, siding turnouts, and car spotting.
    - **Zoom 16** ($380\text{ m} – 900\text{ m}$, $\approx 1.95\text{ m/px}$): Station districts and industrial branch lines.
    - **Zoom 15** ($900\text{ m} – 2,200\text{ m}$, $\approx 3.9\text{ m/px}$): Subdivisions and mountain gorges.
    - **Zoom 14** ($2,200\text{ m} – 5,500\text{ m}$, $\approx 7.8\text{ m/px}$): Multi-town corridor view.
    - **Zoom 13** ($5,500\text{ m} – 14,000\text{ m}$, $\approx 15.6\text{ m/px}$): Broad subdivision corridor view.
    - **Zoom 12** ($> 14,000\text{ m}$, $\approx 31.2\text{ m/px}$): Full route overview covering the entire region.
  - **Pure Mathematical Hysteresis (5% Guard Band):** Eliminates zoom boundary oscillation and flickering when panning or zooming near threshold sizes.
- **Non-Blanking Zoom Transitions:**
  - Tiles from the previous zoom level remain rendered underneath while newly requested zoom tiles load asynchronously in the background. Once new tiles are ready ($\ge 70\%$), previous zoom tiles smoothly deactivate, eliminating jarring black flashes or visual blanks.
- **Active-Aware LRU Texture Cache & Recycled Object Pool:**
  - In-memory LRU cache expanded to 1,024 textures, with active-aware protection ensuring currently visible on-screen tiles are never prematurely evicted or destroyed.
  - Recycled GameObject sprite renderer pool expanded to 384 instances on Unity layer `Layers.Map`, with full sprite and transform sanitization.
- **Adaptive Texture Upload Budget:**
  - Dynamically throttles texture uploads to a maximum of 24 tiles per frame within a strict 5ms main-thread frame budget, maintaining smooth 60+ FPS gameplay during fast pans and zooms.
- **Non-Blocking Asynchronous Discovery & Rescan:**
  - Save-screen hitching completely resolved: tile directory inspections are non-blocking on world load.
  - Added in-game **"Rescan & Reload Tiles"** button that scans thousands of tiles on background threads and instantly refreshes the active grid.
- **Dual-Tier Local Tile Discovery & Robust Format Support:**
  - Supports both standard hierarchical slippy (`{z}/{x}/{y}.png`, `{z}/{x}/{y}.tile`) and flat (`{z}-{x}-{y}.png`, `{z}-{x}-{y}.tile`) structures.
  - Automated fallback to newest validated Railroader native GIS session cache for OpenStreetMap tiles.
  - Non-destructive 4-byte PNG magic header validation (`0x89 0x50 0x4E 0x47`) ensures corrupted files are skipped without altering or deleting user data.
- **Automatic Settings Migration:**
  - Legacy configuration values (Mode 2 = USGS Topo, Mode 3 = OpenStreetMap) seamlessly migrate to Mode 2 (`Map Tiles`) with their respective `MapTileFolder` (`USGSTopo` or `OSM`) automatically preserved.

### 3. 🛤️ CTC Passing Siding Route Propagation (Mainline vs. Siding Accuracy)

In Railroader 0.6 signaled CTC territory, passing sidings consist of multiple track segments between the entrance and exit turnouts. Previously, only the immediate turnout segment was detected as diverging, causing intermediate siding segments to be erroneously promoted to Mainline Green.

<img width="836" height="425" alt="image" src="https://ik.imagekit.io/nbe76wmno/MapEnhancer/Features/RRME2.0-TrackPhysicalSpeedOverlay1.png" />

- **Siding BFS Propagation Engine:** Added contiguous route tracing in `MainlineDetectionEngine.cs`. When a siding turnout leaves confirmed mainline track, all connected intermediate siding segments within the corridor span are identified and assigned proper siding classification (Branch Blue).
- **Corridor Integrity:** Only uncontested through-trackage is promoted to confirmed Mainline Green.

### 13. 🚃 Rolling Stock Marker Dimension Customization & Trim

When cars are coupled in a train or parked on closely spaced parallel tracks in yards, map icons previously collided or overlapped, causing visual fighting and blurred boundaries.

- **End Seam Trim Slider (`CarMarkerLengthTrim`):**
  - Adjusts coupler and end-of-car margins ($70\% – 100\%$, default: $92\%$).
  - Eliminates overlapping end-caps and edge fighting between coupled cars in a consist.
- **Side Flank Trim Slider (`CarMarkerWidthTrim`):**
  - Adjusts lateral side margins ($70\% – 100\%$, default: $90\%$).
  - Ensures cars parked side-by-side in classification yards (such as Bryson City or Andrews) fit neatly without lateral clipping.
- **In-Game Settings Sliders:** Tunable in real-time under the Map Enhancer settings menu (`Ctrl+M`) with immediate visual feedback on the map.

### 14. 🔢 Consist Car Shingling & Alternating Destination Contrast

Long train consists carrying multiple cars assigned to the same destination (or unassigned cars sharing default colors) previously blended into a solid continuous bar, making car counts difficult at dispatch zoom levels.

<img width="1124" height="822" alt="Alternate Shade and Car Highlighting" src="https://ik.imagekit.io/nbe76wmno/MapEnhancer/Features/RRME2.0-CarSize_Selecthighighlight.png" />

- **Consist Depth Filing (Shingling):**
  - Consist cars are topologically indexed from lead to rear with sequential elevation depth offsets ($Y = 3000\text{f} + \text{consistIndex} \times 0.15\text{f}$).
  - Because the top-down map camera looks downward, each subsequent car's leading edge renders cleanly over the preceding car's tail, creating crisp, dark division seams across the entire train.
- **Alternating Consist Colors (Zebra Contrast):**
  - When consecutive cars in a consist share the same destination area color (or unassigned white), odd-indexed cars automatically render in an $18\%$ darker value shade (`v * 0.82f`) while $100\%$ preserving hue and saturation.
  - Allows dispatchers to count cars in a destination block at any zoom level without opening the consist inspector.
- **In-Game Setting Toggle (`AlternatingConsistColors`):**
  - Can be toggled on or off at will under Settings (`Ctrl+M`).

### 15. ✨ Selected Equipment Map Highlighting (Cars, Locomotives & Tenders)

Easily locate and track active equipment on the map when switching or inspecting trains:

- **Pulsating Golden Halo:**
  - Selected equipment is highlighted on the map with a high-contrast, pulsating golden outline (`UnityEngine.UI.Outline` with animated breathing alpha).
- **Combined Locomotive + Tender Pairing:**
  - Selecting a steam locomotive automatically highlights both the locomotive and its coupled tender together as a unified mechanical unit.
  - Selecting the tender directly also highlights its coupled locomotive.
- **Vertical Altitude Boost:**
  - Selected cars receive a $+0.5\text{f}$ vertical altitude boost while selected, ensuring the highlight outline floats above neighboring equipment in the consist without clipping or occlusion.
- **Independent Settings Toggles:**
  - `HighlightSelectedCar`: Toggle selection highlighting for freight cars, passenger coaches, and cabooses.
  - `HighlightSelectedLocomotive`: Toggle selection highlighting for locomotives and tenders.

### 16. 🛡️ User Settings & Map Tile Preservation

Updating or reinstalling Map Enhancer no longer risks losing personalized configuration or downloaded map tiles:

- **Preserved `settings.json`:** Build and packaging scripts (`package.ps1`) automatically detect and preserve existing user settings, custom keybindings, and color preferences.
- **Preserved Tile Directories:** Dedicated provider folders (`Tiles/USGSTopo/` and `Tiles/OSM/`) and custom background images (`Backgrounds/`) are preserved during updates and clean deployments.
- **No Overwrite on Deploy:** Existing player configurations remain 100% intact.
